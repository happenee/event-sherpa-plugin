# Fixing an invoice error

> What to do when the buyer entered wrong billing details and needs a corrected invoice.

> [!info] Overview
> The buyer entered wrong billing details (Company ID, VAT ID, address, company name) and needs a corrected invoice. You can fix the details yourself in the order detail, even after the invoice has been issued. The correction overwrites the details on the order's existing invoices under the same number; it does not issue a new invoice and does not send it to the buyer again.

## Getting started

Open **Orders** — from the *Tools* dropdown at the bottom of the left menu, from the tile on the event **Overview**, or with the button in the top right of the *Tickets* page. Find the order by the buyer's name, e-mail, or company and expand it with the arrow at the end of its row.

## Details

### How to fix the billing details

1. In the order detail, click **Edit billing details**.
2. In the **Billing details** form, correct or fill in *Company*, *Address*, *City*, *ZIP / Postal code*, *Country*, *Company ID*, *VAT ID*, and the *Note* if you need one.
3. Save. The corrected details carry over to the order and to all of its invoices.
4. Click **Download invoice**. You get the same invoice under the same number, now with the corrected details.
5. Send it to the buyer yourself. The system does not send it again.

### What the correction changes and what it does not

- **Changes** the customer details and the note on the order and on all of its invoices.
- **Does not change** the invoice number, issue date, line items, prices, or VAT. You cannot fix the text of a line item with this form.
- **Does not send** the corrected invoice to the buyer. The PDF they received by e-mail earlier keeps the original details.
- **Does not affect** the payment status or the attendee data.

### Limits

- Invoices cannot be deleted or renumbered manually.
- A **paid** order cannot be deleted. You can request a refund from the order detail if no refunded amount has been recorded and the order meets the other refund conditions. Once any refunded amount is recorded, the main button in the order list is hidden, including after a partial refund or a refund reduced by a fee.
- An **unpaid** order can be **canceled** or **deleted** right in the detail.
- A **registered** attendee's data is managed in the **Registration** section, not in the order.

### Prevention

- For repeat clients, prepare consistent billing details (Company ID, address) up front and check them on entry.
- Make the correction as soon as possible after the purchase. The longer you wait, the more likely the buyer has already booked the original PDF.

## Common pitfalls

### Expecting a new invoice or a corrective document after the correction

The correction only overwrites the details on the existing invoice under the same number. It does not issue a new invoice or a corrective tax document. If the buyer or their accountant needs a formal corrective document, editing the billing details will not solve it; contact Happenee customer support.

### The buyer wants the invoice made out to a different company

The form does not stop you from changing the customer to a different company. The invoice keeps its number and issue date, though, and only gets a new customer. Whether that change is acceptable for accounting on an invoice that has already been issued is something to check with Happenee customer support first.

### The buyer wants to fix the attendee's name together with the invoice

Billing details and attendee data are two separate sources. The *Billing details* form changes only the billing details of the order and its invoices; you fix a registered attendee's name in the **Registration** section.
